01Working philosophy

Sensing. Modeling. Translating.

Over the last decade and half, having worked across biological scales, and, along the way learning from stalwarts, I have imbibed the working philosophy of sensing by gathering meaningful data, modeling to build methods, tools and digital twins, for translating into decisions, therapies, education, or a next better question.

This has required staying comfortable in both the wet lab and the dry lab, and making space for clinicians, engineers, students, and collaborators to shape the work.

03Research index

Areas of work.

My work has moved between microbial communities, human health, industrial biology, and learning the language of biological data. Encoding and Decoding the biological world is perhaps the best summarisation of my broader interest.

01

Microbiome & host health

Upper-gut health, dyspepsia, women’s health, bacterial vaginosis, perinatal health, skin microbiomes, and disease-associated dysbiosis.

02

Methods & representations

Microbiome and molecular foundation models, cellular function simulation, biological network inference, and explainable modeling and machine learning.

03

Environment & One Health

Microbial ecology, metabolic agility, nutrient-resilient probiotics, bioremediation, and the relationships linking microbes, environments, and health.

04

Biological Data Science

Genomic NLP, clinical data integration, biomarker discovery, cheminformatics, reproducible workflows, and tools that make evidence easier to inspect and use.

05Profile

I am a Senior Scientist and Principal Investigator at Tata Research Development and Design Centre of TCS Research. I joined TCS Research in 2013 and have grown from researcher to scientific lead while remaining hands-on with experiments, data, models, writing, and of course the diversity of people around the work we do.

My PhD at CSIR-IGIB focused on upper-gut health through metagenomic, clinical health-record, and biochemical data integration. I also serve as Guest Faculty at IIT Delhi and Courtesy Faculty at Florida A&M University.
